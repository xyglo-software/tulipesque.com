Tulipesque VOF is a Dutch company with a British heart. Registered with Chamber of Commerce number 71793542.

We provide advisory, consultancy and training for museums and commercial organisations.

Our core values include:

* Collaboration: We believe in fostering open and collaborative relationships with our clients and partners.
* Transparency: We value transparency in all our interactions, providing clear and honest communication.
* Continuous Learning: We embrace a culture of continuous learning, constantly seeking new knowledge.
* Respect: We respect and value diverse perspectives and creating an inclusive environment.
* Innovation: We embrace innovation, exploring new ideas and technologies to push the boundaries of what is possible.
* Flexibility: We understand that every client and project is unique, and we adapt our approach to meet individual requirements.
